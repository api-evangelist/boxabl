# BOXABL

> BOXABL manufactures factory-built, foldable homes that ship nationwide and
> unfold on site. The company is based in Las Vegas, Nevada and trades on Nasdaq
> under the ticker BXBL.

BOXABL Inc. was founded in 2017. Its homes are built on an assembly line in a
climate-controlled factory rather than on site, which is what makes them faster
and cheaper to produce than conventional construction. Over 700 Casitas have
been built to date.

## Products

The Casita is the current production model, sold in three sizes. Each is
available either as a unit only or as a turnkey package that includes delivery,
installation and appliances — hence the price ranges below.

### Casita Studio

- Floor area: 361 sq ft
- Dimensions: 19ft x 19ft
- Details: https://www.boxabl.com/learn-more/casita-studio
The original house in a box, now in studio, 1 and 2 bedrooms for your backyard or weekend escape with dress-style architectural kits to match your style.

## Where BOXABL can be installed

Homes currently ship to: Alaska, Arizona, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Hawaii, Kansas, Nevada, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Vermont, West Virginia, Wyoming.

Approvals expand over time. Buyers outside an approved state can still start an
order and are notified when their state is added.
